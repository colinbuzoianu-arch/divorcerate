What this is, and what it isn’t.

It is

  • A structured self-reflection that helps you see patterns you may already feel.
  • Grounded in research themes such as the Four Horsemen, repair attempts and attachment.
  • A starting point for a conversation with your partner or a professional.

It isn’t

  • A diagnosis or a prediction. No quiz can tell you whether you will break up.
  • Therapy. It doesn’t replace a qualified couples therapist.
  • A verdict on your partner. It is one perspective: yours.

If you feel afraid of your partner or unsafe at home, a quiz is not what you need right now. Confidential help is available.

Questions, answered.

Is this scientifically validated?

No, and we won’t pretend otherwise. The questions draw on well-established research themes, and the scoring method is published on our methodology page. It’s a tool for reflection, not a validated clinical instrument.

What happens to my answers?

They’re used only to create your result. We don’t sell data and you don’t need an account. Our privacy policy lists exactly what is stored, for how long, and which providers process it.

Can we take it together?

Yes. Take it separately and compare your results. Side-by-side couple comparison is coming soon, and the differences are often more revealing than the scores.

Is it a replacement for couples therapy?

No. Many people use their result to decide whether to seek a therapist, or to start that first session with something concrete.

Why is the full report paid?

The free result gives you the headline. The full report explains every dimension and gives you specific next steps. That’s the part that costs us to produce, and the part that helps you act.
